\section{Battery Degredation}
Battery degradation is represented inside the MILP by the J-segment cycle aging cost
Every dispatch schedule is additionally valued after the fact by rainflow cycle counting 
The rainflow cycle counting result is not used for the optimisation as it is non-linear. However it is more accurate than the piece-wise estimation so is used for calculating the final resulting degredation.
